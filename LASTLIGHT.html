THE LAST LIGHT

TRANSMISSION RECOVERED

Deep-space carrier Lastlight lost contact with the fleet six days ago. Then the hull began to hum.

The Shards are crystalline things that tear through reality itself, opening rifts in the bulkheads and spilling into the reactor deck. They are drawn to energy, and the ship's reactor core is the last thing still burning.

Stand near the core and its field will mend your wounds and slow them down. Step away from it and you are on your own.

Every rift in the hull brings something worse. Collect supply drops, choose your upgrades, and defend the core for as long as you can.
